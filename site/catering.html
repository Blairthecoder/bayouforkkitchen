Good to know

Catering FAQs

What areas do you cater?

We cater across Houston, Southwest Houston, Manvel, Pearland, Missouri City and Sugar Land. If your event is outside these areas, contact us and we’ll let you know if we can make it work.

Do you cater private parties?

Yes. We cater birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, family gatherings and other private parties of nearly any size.

Do you offer corporate catering?

Yes. We handle office lunches, meetings and corporate appreciation events with drop-off or on-site setup.

How far in advance should I inquire?

The sooner the better. Larger orders generally need at least one day’s notice, and full-service events should reach out further in advance so we can plan around your date and venue.

Can I request specific menu items?

Yes. Tell us what you’d like from our menu, along with any dietary restrictions or allergies, and we will build your catering menu around it. Pork is used in our kitchen.
