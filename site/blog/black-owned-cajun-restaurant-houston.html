A Black-Owned Cajun & Creole Restaurant in Houston

  • Home
  • Blog
  • A Black-Owned Cajun Restaurant
By Penny October 20, 2026

Bayou Fork Kitchen is a Black-owned, family-run Cajun and Creole restaurant led by Penny and her son, Chef Blake. From our kitchen at 10600 Fondren Road, we serve Southwest Houston the comfort food our family has spent years building, refining and sharing.

A mother-and-son restaurant story

The business began when Penny left a corporate career and teamed up with Blake to turn a corner store into Mardi Gras Memphis in 2015. Their path later included the French Quarter Food Truck and The Quarter Kitchen + Bar before family brought them to Houston. Bayou Fork Kitchen opened on Fondren Road in September 2026 as the next chapter of that work.

Penny leads the business; Blake leads the kitchen

Penny brings the operating experience that keeps a family restaurant moving. Blake brings more than a decade of professional kitchen experience, including training at Emeril's. Together, they shape a restaurant where the menu and the way guests are treated come from the same family vision.

Cajun, Creole and Southern comfort on one menu

The menu moves from Louisiana favorites such as crawfish étouffée, gumbo and a fried shrimp po-boy to Southern plates such as meatloaf, fried pork chops and red beans with sausage. Sides like dirty rice, mac and cheese, greens and candied yams make it easy to build the kind of meal that feels familiar.

What our Houston guests are saying

As of October 20, 2026, Bayou Fork Kitchen has earned 46 five-star Google reviews. Recent guests have praised the Creole food, catering and service, with perfect marks for food, service and atmosphere. That early support matters deeply to a new independent restaurant, and it helps more Houston diners find us.

How to support Bayou Fork Kitchen

Visit for breakfast, lunch or dinner, use the drive-thru, order pickup or delivery, or ask us about Cajun and Creole catering. If you have eaten with us, an honest Google review helps tell other local diners what to expect.

Come see us on Fondren Road

We are open Monday through Saturday from 6:30am to 7:30pm and closed Sunday. Start with our full menu, read the first-timer's guide, or stop by 10600 Fondren Road in Southwest Houston.
