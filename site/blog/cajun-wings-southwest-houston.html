Cajun Wings in Southwest Houston

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Cajun Wings in Southwest Houston
By Penny August 5, 2026

If you are searching Southwest Houston for real Cajun wings, here is what you need to know: our Sweet Heat Wings are crispy, tossed in a glossy house sweet-heat sauce, and available every day we are open — no special wing night required.

What makes them Cajun wings

It comes down to the seasoning and the sauce. Instead of a straight buffalo or barbecue glaze, our wings get a Cajun-forward sweet-heat treatment: sweetness up front, a savory Cajun kick right behind it. They are fried to order, so they come out hot and crispy, not steamed and soft.

Plate or party tray

Order a plate for lunch or dinner with your choice of one side, or go bigger with a party tray if you are feeding a group. Planning something larger — a birthday, a game day, an office party? Our catering team can build a wing tray around your headcount.

Drive-thru, pickup and delivery across Southwest Houston

We are at 10600 Fondren Road, open every day from 6.00am to 4.00pm and 5.00pm to 10.00pm. Drive-thru is open every service hour, and we deliver out to Manvel, Pearland, Missouri City and Sugar Land in addition to Houston pickup. Same-day orders need to be in before 11.00am.
