Authentic Lake Charles Cajun Food in Houston

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Lake Charles Roots
By Penny November 7, 2026

Bayou Fork Kitchen is a family kitchen with its roots in Lake Charles, Louisiana, now cooking on Fondren Road in Southwest Houston. Everyone in our family is from Lake Charles, and every recipe we serve comes from there. We say authentic, not inspired, because this is the food we grew up eating.

Lake Charles in the kitchen

Southwest Louisiana eats gumbo all year, not just when it turns cool, and it is known for boudin and for home-style Cajun cooking. That is the standard we cook to. Our menu leans on the dishes we know best: gumbo, crawfish étouffée, shrimp and grits, red beans and sausage and dirty rice. Not sure how they differ? Read gumbo, jambalaya and étouffée, explained or Cajun vs. Creole.

A family, and a Tigers family

Our family also has deep LSU roots. Two of our sons are LSU graduates, and one was an All-American track athlete for the Tigers. That is why we love catering for LSU fans in Houston and offer special catering deals for LSU events. See our LSU watch party catering page.

From Lake Charles to Southwest Houston

Our family has cooked in several kitchens over the years; read the full journey on our About page. Today we are at 10600 Fondren Road, serving breakfast, plates, wings and catering to a neighborhood that welcomed us. If you grew up in Louisiana, or you just want to know what it tastes like, come see us.

Come eat, or let us cater

Browse the full menu, order online, or request a catering quote for your next gathering. We are open Monday to Saturday, 6:30am to 7:30pm.
