Catering a Private Event in Houston

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Catering a Private Event
By Penny July 22, 2026

Planning a private event in Houston and thinking about Cajun and Creole catering? Here is how we approach it at Bayou Fork Kitchen, and what to have ready before you reach out.

Tell us about the event first

Every quote starts with the basics: guest count, date, venue, and service style — drop-off, buffet with setup, family style, or full plated service. We cater private events, corporate gatherings, birthdays, weddings and holiday parties across Houston, Manvel, Pearland, Missouri City and Sugar Land.

Build the menu around your crowd

Once we know your guest count, we build a menu around dishes that travel and serve well in quantity — think trays of Sweet Heat Wings, Crawfish Étouffée or gumbo, family meal platters, and sides like dirty rice and mac and cheese. Tell us about dietary restrictions or allergies up front so we can plan around them; pork is used in our kitchen.

Pricing and lead time

Catering packages and tray pricing are still being finalized, so every quote is built around your specific event for now. As a rule of thumb, give us as much lead time as you can — larger orders generally need at least a day's notice, and more for full-service events.

Ready to start?

Request catering pricing through our online form, or call the kitchen directly to talk through your event. For inspiration on what to put on the tray, browse our full menu or read about our Cajun wings in Southwest Houston.
