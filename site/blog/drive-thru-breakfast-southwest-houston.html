Early Drive-Thru Breakfast in Southwest Houston

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Drive-Thru Breakfast
By Penny October 12, 2026

Short answer: Bayou Fork Kitchen opens at 6:30am Monday through Saturday at 10600 Fondren Road, Houston, TX 77096, and the drive-thru stays open every hour we are. Breakfast runs from $3.50 breakfast tacos to $15 shrimp and grits, with chicken and waffles at $14. We close at 6:30pm and are closed on Sunday.

If you need a real breakfast before work and would rather not leave the car, here is what is on our breakfast menu, what each plate costs, and how to pick it up on your way.

The breakfast menu, with prices

  • Breakfast Tacos, $3.50: eggs, cheese, and your choice of sausage or bacon on a soft tortilla.
  • Croissant, $4.00: sausage or bacon, egg and cheese.
  • Bayou Breakfast, $10.00: eggs, sausage or bacon, and a biscuit or toast, with grits or hash brown.
  • Chicken and Waffles, $14.00: crispy fried chicken served over a warm waffle with syrup.
  • Shrimp & Grits, $15.00: creamy grits topped with seasoned shrimp and our signature sauce.

Prices match our DoorDash menu and may change. Final pricing and any fees show at checkout. The full list, with photos, is on our breakfast menu.

Pick your breakfast by appetite

  • Something quick, under $5: a couple of breakfast tacos or a croissant sandwich. Easy to eat in the car.
  • A full plate: the Bayou Breakfast. Eggs, meat, bread, and grits or hash brown, all for $10.
  • Sweet and savory: chicken and waffles.
  • Something Louisiana: shrimp and grits, the Creole version. Read more in where to get shrimp and grits in Houston.

Chicken and waffles in Southwest Houston

Chicken and waffles is a Southern and soul food classic: crispy, salty fried chicken next to a sweet, syrupy waffle. Ours is crispy fried chicken served over a warm waffle with syrup, for $14. It is on our breakfast menu from 6:30am, and it is also listed under Breakfast & Lunch on our full menu, so it is not just a morning order.

How the drive-thru works

Pull into 10600 Fondren Road and order at the drive-thru window. It is open every hour we are, 6:30am to 6:30pm, Monday through Saturday. If you would rather order ahead, place a pickup order on DoorDash, or call the kitchen at 318-403-8968. You can also get breakfast delivered through DoorDash if your address is in its delivery range.

We are on Fondren Road between South Braeswood Boulevard and West Bellfort, in the Brays Oaks area of Southwest Houston, about a mile west of Hillcroft Avenue. If you live or work in Brays Oaks, Westbury, Meyerland or Sharpstown, we are a short drive away. Get directions on Google Maps.

Breakfast for the office

Ordering breakfast for a meeting or a team? Our catering covers office breakfasts and lunches, dropped off or set up on-site across Houston, Manvel, Pearland, Missouri City and Sugar Land. Larger orders generally need at least one day’s notice, so reach out the day before.

FAQ: Breakfast in Southwest Houston

What time does Bayou Fork Kitchen open for breakfast?

Bayou Fork Kitchen opens at 6:30am Monday through Saturday and closes at 6:30pm. We are closed on Sunday.

Is there a drive-thru breakfast spot on Fondren Road?

Yes. Bayou Fork Kitchen at 10600 Fondren Road, Houston, TX 77096 has a drive-thru that is open all service hours, from 6:30am Monday through Saturday.

Where can I get chicken and waffles in Southwest Houston?

Bayou Fork Kitchen on Fondren Road serves chicken and waffles for $14: crispy fried chicken over a warm waffle with syrup. Order at the drive-thru, for pickup, or for delivery through DoorDash.

How much is breakfast at Bayou Fork Kitchen?

Breakfast tacos are $3.50, croissant sandwiches are $4.00, the Bayou Breakfast is $10.00, chicken and waffles is $14.00 and shrimp and grits is $15.00. Prices can change, and the final price shows at checkout.

Can I get breakfast delivered?

Yes. Bayou Fork Kitchen delivers through DoorDash to addresses within its delivery range of our Fondren Road kitchen. Delivery and service fees are set by DoorDash at checkout.
