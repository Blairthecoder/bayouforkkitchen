About us

A melting pot, cooked to order

Our food isn’t just seasoned. It’s layered with creole spice and bold flavor that keeps working on your tastebuds through the whole plate. There are no handed-down recipes here. Everything is our own, and everything starts with butter melting in a pot or oil in the fryer.

Our Story
Who is cooking

The people behind the pot

Blake

Executive Chef. Trained in fine dining at Emeril’s, Blake runs the line and builds the menu. Eleven years cooking professionally, from Mardi Gras Memphis to the French Quarter Food Truck to The Quarter Kitchen + Bar.

Penny

Owner & Operator. A former senior executive who took the leap in 2015 and has run every kitchen since on one rule: amazing service to every customer, every time.

Rounding out the kitchen: two team members work the line alongside Blake and Penny every service.
Our promise

Like your grandmother’s kitchen table

We cook for the person who genuinely savors it: the one who stops mid-bite and asks if that’s garlic they’re tasting. You may have never set foot in southwest Louisiana, but that is where the plate is trying to take you.

What we want you to remember is the whole experience, start to finish, and how we made you feel.
